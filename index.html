Speed Fundamentals

Core habits that shave hours off every run, regardless of class or build.

🧱
Hug the Wall
Default navigation in unknown zones: hug one wall consistently. Confirmed by top speedrunners — reliably leads to exits, bosses, and objectives 9/10 times in PoE2's semi-procedural layouts.
⚔️
Front-Load Damage Nodes
Spend your first 10 passive points exclusively on damage clusters near your class start. Warrior earns ~108% increased melee damage. This functions as a massive multiplier that snowballs kill speed for the entire campaign.
⏩
Keep Moving Always
Inefficient progress beats no progress. Don't stop to study every item. In 0.5, GGG added visual trail cues — follow locusts, blood trails, and signposts toward objectives.
⌨️
Fast Passive Allocation
Hold Ctrl while clicking passive nodes to instantly confirm without the dialog popup. Use Ctrl+Shift+Click to bind weapon sets. Small time saves compound massively across a full run.
💀
Delayed Ascendancy Is Fine
Don't hard-detour for your Ascendancy trial mid-campaign if it breaks momentum. The time lost forcing a detour exceeds most Ascendancy DPS gains in Acts 1–2.
🗡️
Weapon Over Armour
In the early campaign a better weapon almost always provides more effective speed than defensive upgrades. Faster kills = less time taking damage. Check vendors at every town visit.
⚗️
Always Have Two Flasks
A Life Flask and a Mana Flask are mandatory from Act 1 onward. Buy from Una in Clearfell if you don't find them. Flask uptime during boss fights matters more than almost any other variable.
🔮
Consume Skulls Immediately
Gembloom and Gemrot Skulls are permanent +30 Spirit buffs that also generate a Spirit Gem. Consume on the spot — Spirit is the foundational resource for persistent skills and scales with your build for the entire game.
ℹ️
0.5.0 Campaign Changes
Acts 1–3 were rebuilt — all prior speedrun routes are obsolete. The campaign now has shorter areas, smoother Act 3 progression, automatic Waterways pressure pads, and reduced monster density in late sections and Interludes. Visual navigation cues (locust paths, blood trails, signposts) replace memorized layouts.

Crafting Guide
Foundation

Item Anatomy

Understanding what an item is before you craft it. Every expensive mistake traces back to skipping this section.

🆕
0.5 Crafting Changes Overview
Recombinator disabled — the ability to merge mods from two items is gone for this league.
Orb of Dominance does not exist in PoE2 — any guide referencing it is wrong.
Chaos Orb in PoE2 removes one random modifier and replaces it with a new one — it does not reroll all modifiers like in PoE1.
Corruption now multiplies modifier values from their current value instead of rerolling to a random extended range — always Divine to max values first.
🏷️
Rarity Ladder
Normal → Unique
Normal (White) — 0 mods
Magic (Blue) — up to 1 prefix + 1 suffix
Rare (Yellow) — up to 3 prefixes + 3 suffixes
Unique (Orange) — fixed mod pool
Fractured — 1 mod permanently locked
Corrupted — no further changes allowed
⚖️
Prefixes vs Suffixes
3 + 3 on Rare items
Both sides are independent — a slam targeting prefixes never touches suffixes.

Prefixes (rough rule): flat damage, % damage, +gem levels, life, energy shield, mana.
Suffixes (rough rule): resistances, attributes, attack/cast speed, crit chance, recovery. Always verify on PoE2DB before expensive crafts.
📊
Item Level & Tier Gates
ilvl 80–82 for T1 mods
Every modifier has a minimum item level. T1 mods almost always require ilvl 80+. Buy bases at ilvl 81–82 — the cheapest bases that can still roll T1 on the mods you care about.

Greater orbs guarantee modifier level ≥44. Perfect orbs guarantee modifier level ≥70.
🔤
The Tag System
Governs modifier eligibility
Every modifier belongs to a modifier group — only one modifier per group can be on an item at a time. This is why Essence crafts sometimes fail: a mod from the same group is already present.

Pure Strength bases roll Armour mods only. Str/Int hybrids can roll both Armour and Energy Shield. Know your base's tags before you start.
💡
Base Selection Rule
Always choose your base by its modifier pool first, then item level. An ilvl 82 base with the wrong attribute type will never roll the mods you need regardless of currency spent. Hybrid bases (Str/Int, Str/Dex, Dex/Int) give wider mod access at the cost of rarity. Pure bases are cheaper but more predictable.

Currency Index

Every crafting currency in 0.5.0 — what it does, when to use it, and what to hoard.

Recombinator is disabled for the entire Runes of Aldur league. Any Omens of Recombination were deleted on login. The ability to merge mods from two items is gone. All crafts must be planned without it.
Normal-Rarity Conversion
🔵
Orb of Transmutation
White → Magic
Upgrades a Normal item to Magic (1 random prefix or suffix). Starting point for most crafts — needed before Augmentation or Regal can be applied.
Deterministic startHoard early
➕
Orb of Augmentation
Add mod to Magic item
Adds a second modifier to a Magic item that only has one. Use after Transmutation when you want to fill the second mod slot before Regaling.
Targeted useHoard early
⬡
Orb of Alchemy
White → Rare (4 mods)
Transforms a Normal item directly into a Rare item with 4 random modifiers. Skips the Magic step entirely — useful when you want to jump straight to a Rare base without going through Transmute → Augment → Regal. Less targeted than the step-by-step route but faster for throwing together a serviceable item quickly.
Fast but random
↗️
Regal Orb
Magic → Rare
Upgrades a Magic item to Rare, adding one random modifier. You keep both existing mods — the main way to convert a 2-mod Magic item into a crafting base while preserving your good mods.
Targeted upgrade
🔄
Orb of Alteration
Reroll Magic item
Rerolls all modifiers on a Magic item. Used when both mods are garbage. Alt-spam for a specific mod then Regal is a viable early-league strategy.
Spam tool
Rare-Rarity Crafting
🟡
Exalted Orb
Add mod to Rare item
Adds one random modifier to a Rare item with fewer than 6 mods. One of the most important crafting tools — use to fill out a Rare item after locking a key mod. Also the primary trade currency for mid-tier items.
High valueTargeted fill
🎲
Chaos Orb
Reroll Rare item
Removes 1 random modifier from a Rare item and replaces it with a new random modifier. This is a targeted swap — it does NOT reroll all modifiers. Use when one bad mod is dragging down an otherwise good item.
Reroll tool
⬇️
Orb of Annulment
Remove one mod from Rare
Removes one random modifier from all prefixes and suffixes equally. Very dangerous — use Omen of Whittling (Suffix) or Amelioration (Prefix) instead for surgical removal.
High risk
✨
Divine Orb
Reroll modifier values
Rerolls numeric values within their allowed ranges. More common and more necessary in 0.5 — Sanctifying now multiplies from current values, so you must Divine to max before Sanctifying or Corrupting.
More common in 0.5Finisher — use before Corrupt
Precision Tools
🔒
Fracturing Orb
Permanently lock one mod
Permanently locks one random modifier — it can never be removed by any further crafting. Requires 4+ mods on the item. Once fractured, safely Chaos/slam remaining mods without risk of losing the locked one. ~9.5 Ex trade value.
Deterministic lockHigh value

Adds one socket to an item. 10 Artificer's Shards (from salvaging socketed items) = 1 Orb. Sockets cannot be added after Corruption. Priority use: weapon first, then body armour.

Crafting

Runes & Socketables

The most reliable early crafting system — deterministic, predictable, and strong from Act 1 onward. All standard runes were buffed in 0.5.

ℹ️
How Runes Work
Runes grant different bonuses depending on the item type. A Body Rune in body armour grants Life; the same Body Rune in a martial weapon grants Life Leech. Once inserted, a Rune cannot be removed — it's permanent. Add sockets with Artificer's Orbs (10 Shards from salvaging = 1 Orb). Sockets cannot be added after Corruption.
⚡
Socket Priority Order
1. Weapon first — Desert/Glacial/Storm Rune in weapon = highest DPS gain per Orb spent during the campaign.
2. Body Armour second — Body Rune = +60 Max Life at max tier, one of the best defensive stats this early.
3. Gloves — Vision Rune if accuracy-starved. Ring sockets for resistance Runes are low priority vs. finding better gear.
Body Rune
Armour: +30/45/60 Max Life (buffed from +20/30/40)
Martial Weapons: Leech 3/4/5% Physical Damage as Life. Best all-rounder Armour rune — socket this first if nothing more specific is needed.
Mind Rune
Armour: +20/30/40 Max Mana (buffed from +15/25/35)
Staves/Wands: +45/60/75 Max Mana (buffed from +30/40/50). Core rune for mana-hungry spell builds.
Rebirth Rune
Armour: Regen 0.35/0.4/0.45% Max Life/sec (buffed)
Martial Weapons: Gain 15/25/35 Life per enemy killed. Pairs well with high-density maps. The leech sustain rune for melee builds.
Desert Rune
Martial Weapons: Added Fire Damage
One of the best early-campaign weapon runes. Fire damage adds directly to skills without any conversion needed.
Glacial Rune
Martial Weapons: Added Cold Damage
Adds Chill buildup on hits, slowing enemies. Strong on fast-attacking weapons where chill is near-permanent.
Storm Rune
Martial Weapons: Added Lightning Damage
Lightning has the highest average of the three elements. Best raw damage weapon rune on weapons with a wide damage range.
Adept Rune
Armour: +% Spell Damage
Core rune for Intelligence builds — sceptres, wands, and staves benefit most.
Stone Rune
Armour: +% Armour Rating
Scales base Armour multiplicatively. Very strong on high-base Armour gear — pick this for Strength builds that don't need Life or Mana from the socket.
Vision Rune
Armour: +Accuracy Rating
Essential for attack builds that are accuracy-starved. One Glove socket can fix a 10%+ accuracy deficit without sacrificing passive points.
Robust Rune
Armour: +% Energy Shield
Primary rune for ES builds. Put in body armour or helm first — ES scales off base value, so high-base items amplify this rune significantly.
Resolve Rune
Armour: +Evasion Rating
For Dexterity Evasion builds. Evasion has diminishing returns at high values — check your total Evasion Rating before using this over a resistance rune.
Bonded Rune (0.5 Doubled)
Standard: +20 Life and +20 Mana (was +10/+10)
The hybrid life+mana rune — doubled in value in 0.5. Useful when you need both stats and can only spare one socket.

Fracturing Orbs

Lock your best modifier permanently, then craft freely around it without fear of losing it. ~9.5 Exalted Orbs trade value.

🔒
The Core Concept
A Fractured modifier is permanently locked — no currency can remove it. This lets you freely re-roll or slam the remaining mods. The tradeoff: Fracture selects randomly from all eligible mods, so you need 4+ mods and you'll fracture whichever one is randomly chosen — not necessarily your best one. Pre-load the item with your target mod via Essence first, then Fracture.
1
Build to 4+ Modifiers Requirement
Fracturing Orbs require at least 4 mods. Use Essences for your primary target mod first, then fill out the rest. If rolling for the mod naturally, don't Fracture until you have 4 mods you'd be happy with if any of them got locked.
2
Identify Your Target Mod Critical step
Know exactly which mod you'd most want locked. Best fracture targets: T1 Maximum Life, T1 resistance, high weapon damage prefix. Don't Fracture unless you're happy with any of the 4+ mods getting locked.
3
Apply Fracturing Orb ~9.5 Ex cost
One mod is randomly selected and permanently locked (shown with a metallic border). If it's not your primary target, evaluate if the result is still worth continuing — a fractured T1 resistance is still a strong base to build around.
4
Craft Freely Around the Lock
Use Chaos Orbs, Exalted slams, or Essences without risk of losing the fractured mod. Annulment still targets randomly but cannot remove the fractured mod — it can remove others. Use Omen of Whittling/Amelioration for surgical mod removal instead of Annulment.
5
Dominance → Divine → Sanctify/Corrupt Irreversible
Orb of Dominance to upgrade the weakest mod. Then Divine Orb to max all values before Sanctifying or Corrupting — in 0.5 both operations multiply from current values. Corruption is the final irreversible step.

Corruption & Vaal Orbs

The final, irreversible step. Corrupted items cannot be modified by any further crafting currency.

☠️
Corruption Is Irreversible — Always Divine First
Once an item is Corrupted, no currency can modify it further — including adding sockets. Max quality → add sockets → Divine to max values → Corrupt. In 0.5 both Corruption and Sanctification multiply existing values, making pre-Corruption Divining mandatory.
OutcomeEffectRate
Nothing happensItem becomes Corrupted but no changes~25%
Add enchantmentAdds a random enchantment — not prefix/suffix, doesn't count toward 6-mod limit~25%
Add a socketAdds a socket to the item, ignoring the normal socket limit. This is the only way to exceed the item's normal maximum socket count.~25%
Reroll up to 3 modifiers0.5 Change: This outcome now multiplies each affected modifier's value based on its current value (up or down) instead of rerolling randomly within an extended range. Divine to max first — higher starting values mean better expected outcomes.~25%
🆕
0.5 — Multiply, Not Reroll (Corruption & Sanctification)
Both Corruption and Sanctification now multiply each modifier value from its current value rather than rerolling to a random value in an extended range. High current values → higher expected outcomes. Low current values → risk of going even lower. Always Divine Orb to maximum before Corrupting or Sanctifying.

New in 0.5

Aldur's Legacy Rune

Destroys a Unique item and transfers its core ability as a permanent rune. One use per character. Your most important crafting decision in 0.5.

☠️
Permanent and Irreversible — One Per Character
Aldur's Legacy destroys the Unique item you feed it. Each character can only socket one Aldur's Legacy Rune across all equipment. Only activates on Ezomyte or Kalguuran Unique items. Do not rush this — wait until you're deep in maps and fully confident in your build direction.
1
Identify your build's biggest bottleneck
What is your build missing that a Unique item currently provides? Aldur's Legacy lets you transfer that effect to any gear slot. A needed movement skill, aura, or damage modifier from a Unique can move onto your best-in-slot Rare gear.
2
Confirm Ezomyte or Kalguuran type
Only these two Unique subtypes are compatible. Check the item's origin before planning around it.
3
Wait until endgame — do not rush
Early in the league, Unique values and meta priorities shift rapidly. Use the Unique item normally until you're deep in maps and certain of your build direction. Destroying a Unique prematurely for a sub-optimal transfer is the most expensive mistake you can make in 0.5.
4
Socket into your highest-value item
Since you only get one, socket it into your absolute best piece — the item you'll use longest. Body armour, weapon, and helmet are the most impactful slots given their higher socket counts and modifier ceilings.
Craft Strategy

Crafting Workflows

Stage-by-stage crafting sequences for 0.5. Use as a decision tree, not a rigid script.

Standard Rare Item Workflow
1
Select the right base Foundation
ilvl 80+ for endgame T1 mods. Match base attribute type to your build's defensive layer. Buy the cheapest Normal base at the right ilvl.
2
Max quality first Before any crafting
Armourer's Scrap (armour) or Blacksmith's Whetstone (weapons) to 20% quality. Quality cannot be added after Corruption. Most skipped step.
3
Essence for your guaranteed mod One crafted mod rule
Essences work on specific rarities: Normal Essences upgrade a Normal item to Magic with the guaranteed mod. Greater Essences upgrade a Magic item directly to Rare with the guaranteed mod included. Apply to a Normal base for Normal Essences, or a Magic item (with a desirable mod already) for Greater Essences to get a Rare with two known mods.
4
Evaluate remaining mods Decision point
If 2+ remaining mods are useful, continue. If all remaining mods are garbage, Chaos Orb to reroll and repeat from Step 3.
5
Fracture if a second T1 mod appears Optional — high value
If random rolls produced a second high-value mod (T1 Life + T1 resistance on a 4-mod item), apply a Fracturing Orb to lock it. ~9.5 Ex but gives you a permanent double-T1 base.
6
Fill remaining slots with Exalted slams
Exalted Orbs until 5–6 total mods. Check prefix/suffix balance first — a slam fails if the relevant side is full (3/3). Use Omen of Whittling/Amelioration to remove a bad mod on the correct side before slamming.
7
Divine Orb → max all values BEFORE Sanctify/Corrupt
Roll Divine Orbs until all modifier values are at or near maximum. Mandatory before Sanctifying or Corrupting in 0.5.
8
Sanctify or Corrupt — final step Irreversible
Sanctification for the highest possible modifier ceiling. Vaal Orb for an enchantment or socket bonus. Both are irreversible — only do this when the item is fully complete.
⚔️
0.5 Weapon Note — Skill Level Modifiers Reduced
Attack weapons nerfed: +to Melee Skills reduced from +7 to +5 max on 2H, +5 to +4 on 1H. Quiver implicit: +2 to +1.
Spell weapons unchanged: Staffs and Wands retain full values — the best targets for skill-level crafting.
Reallocate attack-weapon crafting budget accordingly.

Common Mistakes

The crafting errors that waste the most currency in 0.5.

❌
Corrupting Before Divining
Most expensive mistake
In 0.5, Corruption multiplies current values. Corrupting a T1 item at 60% roll values locks those in forever. Always Divine to maximum before Corrupting or Sanctifying.
Irreversible
❌
Forgetting Sockets Before Corruption
Sockets cannot be added post-Corruption
Artificer's Orbs cannot be used on Corrupted items. Plan your socket count before the final Vaal Orb. This is the second most common irreversible mistake.
Pre-plan sockets
❌
Stacking Essences (No Longer Possible)
0.5 one-crafted-mod rule
Items can only have one crafted modifier in 0.5. A second Essence application overwrites the first. Chain-Essence crafting is gone — plan your one guaranteed mod carefully.
0.5 change
❌
Using Aldur's Legacy Too Early
One use per character — forever
Destroying a Unique for Aldur's Legacy before your build's endgame direction is clear is the biggest permanent loss available. Wait until you're deep in maps.
Wait for endgame
❌
Chaos Orb Spamming
Inefficient in 0.5
Raw Chaos spam is inefficient — Chaos only swaps one mod, not all. Use Essences for your guaranteed mod and Exalted Orbs to fill remaining slots. Each Chaos Orb application is a single mod replacement, not a full reroll.
Use Essences instead
❌
Skipping Breach
No Catalysts otherwise
Catalysts only come from the Genesis Tree. Skipping Breach means zero Catalyst access and no quality on your jewelry. Engage Breach early and consistently.
Breach is mandatory now
❌
Forgetting Quality
Pre-crafting step
Quality scales base defences multiplicatively with all other modifiers. Skipping 20% quality before crafting or Corruption is a permanent effective stat loss.
Step 2 always
❌
Using Annulment Blindly
Hits random mods
Annulment removes one random mod from all prefixes and suffixes equally. On a 5-mod item, 4/5 chance of losing a good mod. Use Omen of Whittling (Suffix only) or Omen of Amelioration (Prefix only) for surgical removal instead of Annulment.
Use Omens instead
