Speed Fundamentals

Core habits that shave hours off every run, regardless of class or build.

🧱
Hug the Wall
Default navigation in any unknown zone: hug one wall consistently. Confirmed by top speedrunners — this reliably leads to exits, bosses, and objectives 9/10 times in PoE2's semi-procedural layouts.
⚔️
Front-Load Damage Nodes
Spend your first 10 passive points exclusively on damage clusters near your class start. Warrior earns ~108% increased melee damage this way. This functions as a massive multiplier that snowballs your kill speed for the entire campaign.
⏩
Keep Moving Always
Inefficient progress beats no progress. Don't stop to study every item on the ground. In 0.5, GGG added visual trail cues — follow locusts, blood trails, and signposts toward objectives. They reliably lead you forward.
⌨️
Fast Passive Allocation
Hold Ctrl while clicking passive nodes to instantly confirm without the dialog popup. Use Ctrl+Shift+Click to bind weapon sets. These small time saves compound massively across a full campaign run.
💀
Delayed Ascendancy Is Fine
Don't hard-detour for your Ascendancy trial mid-campaign if it breaks momentum. Complete it when it naturally falls in your path. Time lost forcing a detour exceeds most Ascendancy DPS gains in Acts 1–2.
🗡️
Weapon Over Armour
In the early campaign a better weapon almost always provides more effective speed than defensive upgrades. Faster kills = less time taking damage. Check vendors at every town visit; prioritize a damage prefix on your weapon type.
⚗️
Always Have Two Flasks
A Life Flask and a Mana Flask are mandatory from Act 1 onward. Buy them from Una in Clearfell if you don't drop them naturally. Flask uptime during boss fights is more important than almost any other variable.
🔮
Consume Skulls Immediately
Gembloom and Gemrot Skulls are permanent +30 Spirit buffs that also generate a Spirit Gem. Always consume them on the spot — Spirit is the foundational resource for persistent skills and scales with your build for the entire game.
ℹ️
0.5.0 Campaign Changes
Acts 1–3 were rebuilt — all prior speedrun routes are obsolete. The campaign now has shorter areas, smoother Act 3 progression, automatic Waterways pressure pads, and reduced monster density in late campaign sections and Interludes. Visual navigation cues (locust paths, blood trails, signposts) replace memorized layouts.
Act One
I
The Ogham Province
Dark Forests & Haunted Estates
Beach to Count's manor. Follow the corruption through forests, crypts, and blighted farmlands.
⚡
Act 1 Speed Strategy
The Freythorn (+30 Spirit + Spirit Gem L4 from King in the Mists) is the single most important optional in Act 1 — never skip it. For Secrets in the Dark, clear the Red Vale Obelisks before going deep into Grelwood to avoid backtracking. Candlemass (+20 Max Life) is a free kill on the way to Geonor.

King in the Mists
Multi-phase. Watch the debuff color: Purple = keep moving or you'll be frozen. Red (Ritual of Meditation) = stand still or you'll freeze. If standing still is unsafe, build stagger bar and stun him. After kill: consume Gembloom Skull for +30 permanent Spirit + creates Uncut Spirit Gem L4. Return to town, speak to Finn for a Ruby/Sapphire/Topaz Charm (pick your lowest resistance).
  • Crowbell (Hunting Grounds optional) — Follow blood trail to locate it. Defeat for +2 Weapon Set Passive Skill Points. Worth it if you're near its location.

RequiredSkill Gem L5Salvage Bench

Act Boss+20 Max LifeUnlocks Act 2
  • Kill Candlemass first (1st floor, fire boss) → Candlemass' Essence: +20 Maximum Life (permanent). Free power, always do this before Geonor.
Count Geonor — Act 1 Final Boss
Phase 1: Human + wolf hybrid. Bring below 50% HP.
Fog Phase: Arena fills with fog. Kill small monsters for flask charges. Geonor lunges after each voice line — listen for cues, dodge roll sideways.
Phase 2: Full wolf form, new arena-wide AoE abilities. Watch sweep animations. Stay mobile. After kill: town → Hooded One → select "Follow the Beast's trail" to unlock Act 2.

Pursue Countess Oriana. Aid the Ardura Caravan against the corrupted Faradun. More open-ended than Act 1.
⚡
Act 2 Speed Strategy
Unlock Ardura Caravan's waypoint first — it becomes your return hub. Chain the Venom Crypts into the Mastodon Badlands run to avoid backtracking. The Ignaduk boss (Gemrot Skull = +30 Spirit + Gem L10) is your Act 2 equivalent of the Freythorn reward — prioritize it.

WaypointTown + BossSkill Gem L5
  • This is a combat town — monsters spawn nearby. Get the waypoint immediately.
  • NPCs: The Hooded One, Zarka (vendor — check for Act 2 weapons).
  • Quest: Earning Passage — Defeat Rathbreaker at the entrance → Uncut Skill Gem Level 5.
Ardura Caravan → Mastodon Badlands
RequiredWaypoint
  • Find Ardura Caravan — Act 2's hub. Get waypoint. Speak to all NPCs to unlock quests.
  • At least one NPC sells higher-level gems for Act 2 — check inventory.
  • Progress to Mastodon Badlands for main quest objectives. Chain the Venom Crypts on the same run to avoid backtracking.
Ignaduk — Spirit Skull Boss ★ NEVER SKIP
Boss+30 SpiritSpirit Gem L10
Ignaduk
Defeat Ignaduk → loot Gemrot Skull → consume immediately for +30 permanent Spirit + Uncut Spirit Gem Level 10. This is the biggest power jump in Act 2. Return Ignaduk's Ghastly Spear to Servi in town for a Magic Rarity Charm — select based on your lowest resistance or best effect for your build.
Ancient Ruins → Sandworn Road
RequiredWaypoints
  • Chain these zones efficiently — grab each waypoint as you pass through.
  • Sandworn Road leads to the Act 2 final area. Follow main quest markers.
  • Resistance checks become critical here — stop at Caravan to gear up if under-resisted.
  • Side quest encounters in this region often reward Passive Skill Points — do them if they appear near your current path.

Act 2 Final Boss — Multi-phase Chaos/Poison
Stay mobile throughout. Avoid standing in any ground effects — they stack debuffs quickly. Use flasks aggressively, especially during phase transitions. After the kill, return to the Hooded One to unlock Act 3 and transition forward.
Act Three
III
The Ziggurat & Drowned City
Ruins, Cannibals & The Apex of Filth
Corrupted city ruins with cannibal hordes. Streamlined in 0.5 — Waterways is now fully automatic.
⚡
Act 3 Speed Strategy (0.5 Update)
0.5 smoothed Act 3 significantly. Waterways pressure pads are now automatic — just walk through. Monster density is reduced in later areas. The main threats are the drowning orb mechanic in Drowned City and the Queen of Filth's mobility. Check vendors at Ziggurat Encampment for resistance fills before pushing forward.
Ziggurat Encampment — Town
WaypointTown Hub
  • Vendors stock Act 3-tier gear. Check immediately for weapon and resistance upgrades.
  • Target: capped Cold/Fire/Lightning resistance + a DPS weapon competitive with your level before pushing forward.
  • Speak to all NPCs to unlock available quests.
Waterways (Streamlined in 0.5)
RequiredWaypoint
  • Patch 0.5: Pressure pads are now automatic — just progress normally through the zone.
  • Navigate to the zone exit and push forward toward Apex of Filth.
The Drowned City
RequiredWaypoint
  • Cannibals, colossal volatile enemies (explode on death — move away during death animation), and the drowning mechanic.
  • Drowning Orb: Moving teal orb that reduces vision. Stand in it too long = death. Always move away immediately when it approaches you.
  • Primary objective: reach the Apex of Filth.
Apex of Filth — Act 3 Final Boss
Act BossUnlocks Act 4
  • Open runic zone — cannibals, volatile enemies, shamans, Filthy Crones (curse-based, physical projectiles).
Queen of Filth — Act 3 Final Boss
Physical and chaos themed. Extremely mobile — repositions constantly, requiring you to track her movement. Casts circular cursed ground patches — never stand in these (they apply debuffs quickly). Use Dodge Roll to avoid patches while staying on her. After kill, progress to Act 4 transition.
Act Four
IV
Kingsmarch & The Karui Archipelago
Non-Linear Island Exploration
Island-hop from Kingsmarch. Collect quest pieces, recruit allies, complete The Search to unlock Interludes.
⚡
Act 4 Optimal Route — Speed + All Permanent Rewards
Act 4 is non-linear but has a clear optimal path. The route below minimizes travel by chaining geographically close islands and ensures every permanent buff and passive point is collected. The Abandoned Prison flask buff (Life or Mana Recovery) is flexible and can be swapped later for free, so don't stress the choice.
